01 / The premise

The next era of AI will be designed together.

As AI’s computational and energy demands accelerate, progress requires more than capable models. This workshop brings researchers, engineers, open-source developers, and industry partners together to rethink how AI systems are designed, built, and deployed.

View the event on Indico ↗

Convergent intelligence

Algorithms, hardware, software architectures, design tools, and data resources developed in close coordination—enabling AI that is more efficient, sustainable, accessible, and scalable.
